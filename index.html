What is it?

MemGallery is a smart, local-first memory bank designed to store everything you encounter—audio, images, text, and URLs—so you can recall them exactly when you need them. It acts as an intelligent index for your digital life, ensuring no breakthrough thought or essential screenshot is ever lost again.

Why build it?

The inspiration came from powerful manufacturer-locked features like OPPO's Mind Space, Nothing's Essential Space, and Pixel Screenshots. These are incredible tools, but they are trapped within specific ecosystems. MemGallery was born to be the open-source alternative—a device-agnostic intelligence engine that brings that same power to everyone, with no manufacturer lock-in.

How it works?

We’ve built a seamless pipeline that prioritizes your privacy while maximizing your recall.

  • CAPTURE: Snap screenshots, record voice notes, or share URLs directly to the app.
  • INDEX: Proactive AI (Gemini, Groq, or Local) scans, transcribes, and extracts actions.
  • RECALL: Use natural language to search your entire history and act on detected tasks instantly.

Save more than just text. MemGallery handles your images, voice recordings, and web bookmarks effortlessly.
